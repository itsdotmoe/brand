# brand

Brand guidelines for moe.

## Naming

Our name is **moe**. Always lowercase. Not "Moe", "MOE", or any other casing.

The trailing **e** of the wordmark is rendered in [Moe Pink](#brand-colors). When the wordmark appears in a single color, the pink `e` is preserved.

The icon is **m.** — the letter in the body color, the period in Moe Pink (a nod to `.moe`).

## Usage

Do not alter the brand assets in any way, or display them in a way that implies a relationship with moe.

Maintain clear space around the wordmark equal to the height of the `e` on all sides. Do not place the wordmark over busy imagery or colors that compromise legibility.

## Brand Colors

| Name | Hex | RGB |
|------|-----|-----|
| Moe Pink | `#ff4fa1` | 255, 79, 161 |
| Moe Pink Light | `#ff75b8` | 255, 117, 184 |
| Moe Black | `#0e0c0f` | 14, 12, 15 |
| White | `#ffffff` | 255, 255, 255 |

Moe Pink is the canonical accent. Moe Pink Light is its hover / lighter tier. Moe Black is the canonical background.

## Typography

Wordmark is set in the system sans-serif at weight 700 with `-0.025em` letter-spacing. On Windows this resolves to Segoe UI Variable; on macOS, SF Pro; on Linux, the platform's UI font.
